# Saved Searches

Save your favorite search queries and filters for quick access later.

## How to use

1. Perform a search and apply filters.
2. Click **Save Search** and give it a name.
3. Access saved searches from your user menu.

![Saved Searches Screenshot]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/features/saved-searches.png)

## Benefits

- Quickly re-run complex queries.
- Share saved searches with your team (coming soon).
